# Company profiles and offerings — local review

Latest founder direction: adapt the hotel/company tooling into company-owned profiles with website links and relevant offerings (activities, events, products). Preserve the guest campaign and social-first roadmap; do not make users repeat a business quiz.

This increment reuses the fictional hotel's catalog through a small adapter and the existing catalog ranker. `?company=demo-hotel-clt` is a dark Tasteprint company example. `?companyAdmin=1` edits fictional profile/website details and structured posts. Save → `?company=demo-hotel-clt&preview=1` shows the saved browser draft. An explicit import copies selected offers from the local hotel Studio draft after confirmation. The original campaign, Studio and Workspace are retained.

Relevant-only view uses explicit Passport interests without transmitting them to a company. Browse-all remains available, respecting personal avoids and expired/withdrawn offerings. Verification/partner metadata never buys organic ranking. Events require timezone-bearing start/end metadata and expire; products do not inherit event dates. Unknown prices/access/availability remain unknown. Fictional posts do not contaminate real Discover, Together or Passport history, and cannot be booked or mistaken for actual business inventory.

**Verification is not active.** Browser input cannot self-certify company ownership, source freshness or availability. A future reviewed Workspace-to-company ownership claim may confirm who controls the official website. It must not certify every offering or override independent relevance/nonpartner eligibility. Hosted posting requires authenticated tenant-scoped write/review/publish permissions and source validation; these have not been activated. Publishing requests fail unavailable, never fall back to private drafts. Draft preview links work only in the browser containing the draft.

Architecture: `company-core.js` adapts only reviewed fictional catalog fields (no guest answers, leads, scoring or private data), normalizes posts and reuses `rankCatalog`; `company-storage.js` is bounded local-only storage; `company.js` provides an accessible structured editor/view; `company.css` uses the dark tokens. Existing Business entry links to the example. HTTPS links reject embedded credentials and unsafe schemes; output is escaped. Anonymous telemetry is disabled for these routes. Editor supports export and explicit local deletion; validation/storage failures retain unsaved input.

Validation: `test:company`, full `npm test` / build, and `test:browser` covering both root and Pages with the added company suite. Synthetic coverage includes no self-verification, no PII/quiz import, immutable campaign input, relevance vs browse-all, hard avoids, expiry/timezones, unknown product fields, partner-rank invariance, private preview isolation, HTML/URL injection, storage errors and mobile widths. Real company verification, content rights, business publishing and hosted source freshness remain unverified and require separate approval.

Next consumer milestone remains server-authoritative private invitations/plans; company production participation comes after usefulness is demonstrated. No live service, deployment, paid account, real company enrollment, business contact or personal-data collection was initiated.
